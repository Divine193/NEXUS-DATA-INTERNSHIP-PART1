# NEXUS-DATA-INTERNSHIP-PART 1 & PART 2
# 📊 Sales Performance Analysis (Excel)

## 📝 Project Overview
> This project analyzes historical sales data to identify seasonal revenue trends, top-performing product categories, and regional growth opportunities.

## 📂 Data Source
Describe where the dataset came from and its structure.
* **Source:** Kaggle
* **Dataset Size:**  1194 records, 12 columns
* **Key Variables:** `Order_Date`, `Product_Category`, `Amount`, `Profit`, `Product_Sub-Category`

## 🛠️ Tools & Techniques
* **Tool:** Microsoft Excel
* **Data Cleaning & Transformation:** Excel Formulas (e.g., `XLOOKUP`, `INDEX/MATCH`, `IF` statements)
* **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** Pivot Tables
* **Data Visualization:** Excel Dashboards, Pivot Charts, and Conditional Formatting

## 🔬 Methodology

1. **Data Cleaning:** Handled missing values, standardized date formats.
2. **Exploratory Data Analysis:** Built Pivot Tables to aggregate yearly sales distributions and calculate profit margins across different states.
3. **Dashboard Creation:** Designed an interactive dashboard on a dedicated sheet using Slicers to allow users to filter data by year and product category.

## 📈 Key Insights
Highlight 3 to 5 major findings. 
* **Insight 1:** Sales & Profit of the store increased from year 2020 to 2023 and starts to decline in year 2024.
* **Insight 2:** Office Supplies & Furniture were the most profitable Categories, though Electronics had more sales than furniture, Phones and Laptops were responsible for this decline in Profit in the Electronics Category .
* **Insight 3:** laptops sales & profits dropped the most in the summer months, it becomes worse further in the years(2024, 2025), that is the reason for the decline in the profits of the Electronics category.
* **Insight 4:** Florida was the most profitable state in the span of five years, while New York & California comes second & third respectively, while New York was the best selling state, with Florida & California following it.

## 💡 Recommendations

* Shift 20% of the Q3 marketing budget to promote high-margin products in Florida.
* Introduce promotional bundling for Laptops & Phones during off-peak summer months to stabilize revenue.
* Also Introduce Promotional Bundling for the products under the furniture category to increase sales in that category.
